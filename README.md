# Community Deprivation Analytics

### Overview

This project was developed as part of a Data Science consultancy project for **Resole**, a UK social enterprise that redistributes footwear to vulnerable communities.

The objective was to identify communities with the greatest levels of need across the United Kingdom by analysing deprivation indicators, population demographics, income statistics, and socio-economic trends. The project aimed to support evidence-based decision making and improve the effectiveness of footwear redistribution programmes.

The work followed the complete Data Science Project Lifecycle, from stakeholder analysis and project planning through to data preparation, exploratory data analysis, dashboard development, forecasting, and recommendations.


### Project Objectives

* Identify areas experiencing the highest levels of deprivation
* Analyse socio-economic factors influencing community need
* Investigate demographic and population trends
* Explore relationships between deprivation indicators and regional demand
* Develop visualisations and dashboards for stakeholder decision-making
* Build forecasting models to support future planning
* Provide data-driven recommendations for resource allocation


### Client Context

Resole is a UK-based social enterprise focused on reducing footwear poverty by redistributing donated footwear to vulnerable groups, including:

* Homeless individuals
* Refugees
* Care leavers
* Children from low-income families
* Community support organisations

The project explored how data analytics could be used to identify areas where support could have the greatest social impact.

### Key Findings

- London boroughs recorded some of the highest homelessness counts.
- Higher deprivation levels were generally associated with increased homelessness.
- Significant variation exists between local authorities in terms of GDHI and demographic characteristics.
- Population density and socio-economic indicators showed measurable relationships with homelessness trends.
- Interactive dashboards enabled effective communication of complex social and economic datasets.
